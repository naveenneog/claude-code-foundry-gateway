# P97 Cosmos entitlement persists until a sync changes it, 2026-10-05

[ADR-0051](../adr/0051-persistent-sync-based-cosmos-entitlement.md) records the decision. This file records
the evidence.

### Why

Since ADR-0017 every Cosmos entitlement record expired 7,200 seconds after the scan that wrote it, and the
resolver answered 503 for an expired record. A 30-minute job, given Graph access by a tenant administrator,
was therefore a dependency of every request:

- a job outage of two hours stopped every developer;
- every run rewrote every record;
- the switch from named values waited 60 to 90 minutes for three runs.

The owner asked for the named-value behaviour on Cosmos:

- access lasts until a sync removes or moves the person;
- syncs run on demand with the operator's own sign-in;
- a sync for one known person reads only that person's memberships.

### Decisions

The binding decisions are ADR-0051 decisions 1 to 9:

- persistent records;
- a cache for `entitlement-cache-seconds`;
- writes of changes only;
- a snapshot apply-by limit;
- `Sync-ClaudeAccess.ps1 -Store` and `-User`;
- a switch without the job or its receipt;
- a runner started when stopped;
- an optional sync job, Manual unless a schedule is passed;
- the resolver's service principal, the region check and `entitlement-projection-prefix` from the deployer.

The streams worked from one written contract, and the lead merged them on `p97-cosmos-sync`.

### Acceptance criteria

1. The resolver answers a record without `expiresAt`, and a legacy record until its `expiresAt` passes,
   and refuses:
   - a legacy record whose `expiresAt` has passed (404; amended in council round 1);
   - status records (404);
   - another tenant (403);
   - an unknown tier (409);
   - a missing, malformed or future generation or verification time (503).
2. The gateway policy checks no expiry. It caches a 200 answer for `entitlement-cache-seconds` and a 404
   for at most 60 seconds, and answers 503 for a resolver failure.
3. A full sync writes added and changed records, deletes orphans, writes no `expiresAt`, rewrites every
   legacy record that carries one, and still refuses when the groups resolve to nobody. A snapshot past its
   apply-by time is refused. A full sync, from a snapshot or the job, leaves out the users whose targeted
   sync finished after its directory read began, and refuses when a newer full sync finished.
4. `Sync-ClaudeProjection.ps1 -ExportPath -User` resolves one person with `checkMemberGroups`, in batches of
   at most 20, with the full sync's precedence. `apply-projection.mjs --user` writes or deletes only that
   person's record.
5. `Sync-ClaudeAccess.ps1` follows `entitlement-source`:
   - on a projection gateway it exports, starts the runner when stopped, applies and reports;
   - `-Store named-value` keeps the named-value behaviour;
   - `-User` with `-Store named-value` is refused.
6. The switch reads no receipt and needs no job. It runs, in order:
   1. resolver checks, including the resolver's service principal;
   2. the drift check;
   3. the runner compare;
   4. evidence: a full sync in the last 24 hours and no record the resolver would refuse;
   5. a backup;
   6. one write.

   A gateway with no named-value entitlement compares the projection with a fresh snapshot instead.
7. The deployer does three things:
   - creates the resolver's service principal when missing;
   - refuses a `-Location` that differs from an existing `cosmos-<prefix>`;
   - writes `entitlement-projection-prefix`.
8. The optional job's trigger is Manual unless `-CronExpression` is passed. Its stale alert exists only
   with a schedule, and its expiry alert is gone.
