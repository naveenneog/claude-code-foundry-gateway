### Why

Read on `d0605cb` on 2026-10-05:

- `scripts/Deploy-ClaudeProjection.ps1:111-216` redeploys Cosmos, the network and the resolver,
  republishes the resolver and applies a fresh snapshot in the same run as
  `-FlipAfterCleanCompare`. Admission then reads live records on a generation newer than the
  job's last status, so every switch attempt is refused (D5; the P94 simulation shows the refusal,
  [P94 status](P94.md#p94-the-p86-renewal-job-deploys-and-renews-2026-10-04)).
- The guided flow's live discovery returns gateway state only
  (`scripts/flow/lib/LifecycleCommon.ps1:125-151`), so `scripts/flow/Entitlement.ps1:80-83`
  refuses every live switch for missing renewal evidence, and the flow runs no compare: it
  records `cleanComparison` without acting on it (`scripts/flow/Entitlement.ps1:71`) (D9).
- `Assert-ClaudeProjectionAdmission` accepts any non-blank action-group id and never reads the
  receivers (`scripts/ClaudeProjectionChecks.ps1:145-147`) (D13).
- Admission checks the form of the job's settings, not that its evidence was produced under them
  or that they name the gateway and groups being switched (P94 council, Architect and Security).
- P84-era text still says the switch is unavailable, in 15 places including a user-facing
  message (`scripts/ClaudeProjectionChecks.ps1:67`) (D10).

### Decisions

[ADR-0050](../adr/0050-projection-switch-function.md) records them.

1. One function, `Invoke-ClaudeProjectionSwitch` in `scripts/ClaudeProjectionSwitch.ps1`, runs the
   switch: drift check and gateway export, read-only runner compare, admission, backup, one
   `entitlement-source` write, and a rollback note. The deployer's `-FlipAfterCleanCompare` runs
   only this function; the installer reaches it through the deployer; the guided flow calls it.
2. The renewal receipt from P94 is the switch's input. The deployer reads
   `onboarding/projection-renewal-<prefix>.json` when the renewal parameters are not given; the
   flow uses the receipt whose `gatewayResourceId` is the discovered gateway, confirmed against ARM.
3. Admission reads the action group from ARM and requires it enabled with at least one email
   receiver whose status is `Enabled` (U119).
4. The job writes its settings into each status record. Admission counts only status records
   written under the job's current settings, and the switch requires those settings to name the
   gateway being switched, the compared tier groups and the receipt's identity.

### Acceptance criteria

1. Given `-FlipAfterCleanCompare`, `scripts/Deploy-ClaudeProjection.ps1` makes no ARM deployment,
   app registration, resolver publish, Cosmos role assignment, snapshot export or apply; its only
   Azure write is the `entitlement-source` update, after the drift check, the gateway export, the
   runner compare, admission and the backup, in that order. A refusal at any step leaves
   `entitlement-source` unchanged and writes no backup. `-WhatIf` runs the reads and admission and
   stops before the backup.
2. Admission refuses an action group that is disabled or has no email receiver with status
   `Enabled`, and names the remedy.
3. Admission refuses status evidence written under other job settings. The switch refuses a job
   whose gateway, tier groups or identity differ from the gateway being switched, the compared
   tier groups or the receipt.
4. The guided flow's live discovery supplies the renewal evidence from the receipt whose
   `gatewayResourceId` matches the gateway, after confirming in ARM that the job exists and runs
   the receipt's digest. A missing, ambiguous or unconfirmed receipt refuses with the remedy. The
   flow's switch runs `Invoke-ClaudeProjectionSwitch`, so the compare always runs.
5. In the simulation, scheduled runs followed by admission under the job's settings pass, and the
   same evidence checked against other settings is refused.
6. After a switch, the output names the backup file and states the rollback: `entitlement-source`
   returns to `named-value` after the lists are refreshed with `scripts/Sync-ClaudeAccess.ps1` and
   compared with `scripts/Compare-ClaudeEntitlement.ps1 -FailOnDrift`.
7. Every P84-era statement listed under Why is replaced with the current behaviour, with
   citations, and the tests that pinned the old text pin the new text.
8. An owner-attended runbook lists each live step from the projection deployment to the switch and
   the rollback, and what each step shows (U17, U109, U113, U116).
